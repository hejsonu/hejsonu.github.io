Even Baskets

Two people are carrying the shopping home and want to pay half each. Split today's eight items into basket A and basket B so both baskets cost exactly the same — and keep the fragile item out of the basket with the heavy one.

Today's shopping

Basket A: $0Basket B: $0

How to play

  1. Each item has a price. Tap A or B next to it to put it in that basket. Tap the same letter again to take it back out.
  2. The running totals of both baskets are shown above the list.
  3. When every item is packed, the two totals must be equal.
  4. The item marked fragile and the item marked heavy must end up in different baskets.

Tips

  • Find the half first. Add up all eight prices and halve it. That is the amount each basket needs. If the items cost $58 in total, each basket must come to $29.
  • Place the rule items first. The fragile and heavy items always go to different sides, so put one in A and one in B before anything else. Now you only need to fill the gaps.
  • Mind odd and even. If one basket is short by an odd amount, you need to move an odd number of odd-priced items. This rules out many swaps quickly.
  • Swap, don't restart. If basket A is $4 too high, look for an item in A and an item in B whose prices differ by $2 and swap them — that changes each side by $2.

Frequently asked questions

Is there always an answer?

Yes. A program checks all 128 different ways to split the eight items (swapping the two baskets is not counted as a new way) and only uses shopping lists with one to three answers. It also makes sure the fragile rule matters: on every board, at least one split with equal totals is ruled out because it would crush the fragile item.

Why do basket A and basket B count as the same answer when swapped?

Because the baskets are identical — moving everything from A to B and back gives the same split. When you win, the count of answers shown ignores these mirror images.

When does a new puzzle appear?

The daily list is based on the date on your own device, so it changes at midnight your time. Everyone on the same date gets the same items.

Can I play more than one a day?

Yes. Practice shopping makes a fresh random list as many times as you like. Back to today returns to the daily one.

Do you save my results or track me?

No. The puzzle runs entirely in your browser. It does not use cookies, does not store anything on your device, and sends nothing to us.

Are the prices real?

No. Prices are whole dollars from $1 to $12 picked by the program to make a good puzzle, not prices from any shop.
