Budget Aisle

A small daily puzzle about spending money exactly. Each square of the store has a price. Walk from the entrance to the checkout, buying one item in every square you pass, and finish with exactly the budget spent — not a dollar more or less.

How to play

  1. You start at the top-left square (marked in). Its price is already in your cart.
  2. Tap a highlighted square to the right of you or below you to walk there. You can never move up or left.
  3. Every square you step on adds its price to your total.
  4. Reach the bottom-right square (marked pay) with a total equal to the budget to win.
  5. Made a wrong turn? Tap Undo, tap any square already in your cart to walk back to it, or use Start over. On a keyboard, use the right and down arrow keys and Backspace.

Tips

  • Every route has the same length. On a 5 by 5 map you always visit 9 squares, so the budget divided by 9 tells you the average price you need. A budget of $54 means you need squares worth about $6 each.
  • Work backwards too. The checkout square is always on your route, so subtract its price from the budget first. Then do the same with the entrance.
  • Watch the "left" counter. If you have $10 left with three squares to go, every remaining square must average a bit over $3. If the cheapest squares ahead are $5, turn back now.
  • Count the corners. The two squares next to the entrance and the two next to the checkout split all routes into a few families. Trying each family once is often faster than guessing.

Frequently asked questions

Is there always an answer?

Yes. Each store is made by first choosing a real route and then setting the budget to that route's total, so at least one route always works. A program also counts every possible route (there are 70 on a 5 by 5 map) and we only use stores where one to three routes hit the budget, so the puzzle is neither trivial nor impossible. When you win, the page tells you how many working routes exist.

When does a new puzzle appear?

The daily store is based on the date on your own device, so it changes at midnight your time. Everyone on the same date gets the same store.

Can I play more than one a day?

Yes. Practice store makes a fresh random store as many times as you like. Back to today returns to the daily one.

Do you save my results or track me?

No. The puzzle runs entirely in your browser. It does not use cookies, does not store anything on your device, and sends nothing to us. Reloading the page simply starts the store again.

Is this good practice for kids?

It is plain addition and estimation with numbers from 1 to 9, so it suits anyone comfortable adding up a shopping list. Saying the running total out loud is a good way to play it together.
