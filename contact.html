Spotted a wrong number, or have an idea for a calculator? Please open an issue on our public GitHub page: github.com/hejsonu/hejsonu.github.io/issues. A free GitHub account is needed to post, and issues are public, so do not include personal information.

When reporting a mistake, please include the page, the values you entered and the result you expected. Confirmed mistakes are fixed on the page. We cannot give financial, legal or medical advice.
