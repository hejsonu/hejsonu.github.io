About Everyday Figures

Everyday Figures is a small independent site of calculators for questions that come up while shopping, cooking, gardening and doing projects around the home. Each page does one job, explains the formula it uses, and includes worked examples so you can check the result yourself.

How the tools are made and checked

The pages and calculators are written with AI tools. Before a page goes up, every worked example on it is recalculated by a separate program using a different method (for example, counting fence posts one by one instead of using the formula) and compared with what the calculator on the page actually shows; if the two disagree, the page is fixed before it is published. Where a topic has no single correct answer (for example, exact baking times), the page says so instead of guessing.

Your data

All calculations run in your browser. The numbers you type are not sent to us or stored. See the privacy policy for details.
